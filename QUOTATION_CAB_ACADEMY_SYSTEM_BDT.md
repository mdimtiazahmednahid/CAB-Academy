## Executive Summary

The **CAB Academy LMS & Executive Education Platform** is an enterprise-grade, high-performance web platform tailored specifically for professional certification programs, corporate cohort training, executive masterclasses, and career placement in Bangladesh. 

Built on the robust **Laravel 11 MVC architecture** with **Tailwind CSS**, **Alpine.js**, and **Vite**, the system includes a conversion-optimized corporate front-end, a student learning experience with interactive quizzes and verifiable certificate issuance, multi-channel payment verification (bKash/Nagad/Rocket/Bank), a recruitment job board, and a comprehensive super-administrative back-office.
