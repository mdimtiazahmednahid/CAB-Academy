# EdTech Growth Marketing & Business Decision Architecture

This skill codifies domain expertise in **EdTech Growth Marketing, Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO), and Business Economics**, specifically tailored for executive, professional, and vocational certification institutions like CAB Academy.

---

## 1. The Core EdTech Conversion Psychology

In professional and executive EdTech, course purchases are rarely impulse buys; they are **career investments** requiring reassurance, credibility, and reduced perceived risk.

### The 4 Pillars of Course Conversion Velocity
1. **Visual Authority & Institutional Trust**:
   - Prospective students and corporate sponsors judge the credibility of a certification within the first 3 seconds of landing.
   - Trust markers (e.g., Recognized Certification, Corporate Client affiliations, NBR/Customs expert faculty) must be strategically placed in the eye-path before financial asks.
2. **Action Clarity (Fitts's Law & Focal Centering)**:
   - The primary Call-to-Action (CTA) must never be hidden in corners or overwhelmed by secondary choices.
   - Centered, full-width or large-target buttons (`min-h-[44px]` touch targets) reduce interaction friction and eliminate decision fatigue.
3. **Scarcity & Schedule Urgency**:
   - Courses run in batches (e.g. `Batch 105th`, `Starts 23 Oct`).
   - Coupling a clear start date with a scarcity badge (`Seats Open` or `Limited Cohort`) drives immediate action over passive bookmarking.
4. **Omnichannel Safety Valves (WhatsApp & Phone)**:
   - In emerging and South Asian markets, 80%+ of high-ticket professional course inquiries require conversational reassurance.
   - Every product card or page must provide a direct path to human counseling (Instant WhatsApp or Direct Hotline) alongside digital checkout.

| **Conversational CTA ("WhatsApp Support")** | `py-2.5 sm:py-3 px-5 font-bold` | Emerald green (`#25D366`); prominently placed in top bar and inquiry sections. | Catches hesitant buyers who need human answers before paying. |

## 3. High-Converting Micro-Copy Guidelines

- **Use Action-Oriented Verbs**:
  - ✅ `Enroll Now`, `Secure Your Seat`, `Explore Courses`, `Instant WhatsApp Inquiry`
  - ❌ `Submit`, `Click Here`, `Read`, `Go`
- **Friction-Reducing Subtext**:
  - Pair checkout buttons with confidence cues: `100% Live Online (Zoom)`, `Official Certificate Included`, `Mirpur-Pallabi Campus Support`.
- **Transparent Price Presentation**:
  - Always pair currency symbols clearly: `9,000.00 ৳` or `Free ৳`.
  - Place fee badge on thumbnail corner where image contrast is darkest.

## 4. Business & Product Decisions Checklist

Before deploying button or layout modifications, audit against these 6 business questions:

- [ ] **Focal Point Test**: Does the user's eye immediately land on the next logical action within 1.5 seconds?
- [ ] **Touch Target Compliance**: Is every button at least `44px` tall on mobile viewports?
- [ ] **Secondary vs Primary Ratio**: Is there a clear 60/40 visual weight hierarchy between primary and secondary buttons?
- [ ] **Social Proof Proximity**: Is there a proof point (corporate clients, alumni count, or verified faculty badge) nearby?
- [ ] **Conversational Fallback**: Can a user who has questions reach a counselor via WhatsApp or phone with a single tap?
- [ ] **Zero Redundancy**: Does the button or micro-copy add new value rather than repeating what the title already states?
